About Me

I am a graduate social work student at Boston University School of Social Work, pursuing a clinical practice concentration with a behavioral health specialization.

My professional interests include psychotherapy, trauma-informed care, mindfulness, emotional regulation, and strengths-based approaches to mental health and well-being.

I value empathy, cultural humility, collaboration, and respect for each individual's experiences and strengths. My goal is to continue developing the knowledge and clinical skills needed to support individuals and communities through meaningful therapeutic relationships.
