Introduction

Hi, I'm Sumit! I'm a Master of Social Work student at Boston University School of Social Work, specializing in behavioral health. I'm currently completing my graduate clinical internship at EMDR Therapy Solutions & Wellness, where I'm continuing to grow as a developing clinician through supervised clinical practice.

What I enjoy most about clinical social work is the opportunity to connect with people, hear their stories, and understand what matters most to them. I believe everyone deserves to feel heard, respected, and supported without judgment. Whether someone is navigating anxiety, depression, trauma, relationship challenges, or a difficult life transition, I value creating a welcoming and collaborative environment where they can feel comfortable being themselves.

My approach is grounded in compassion, curiosity, cultural humility, and a genuine appreciation for each person's unique experiences. I draw from cognitive behavioral, psychodynamic, mindfulness-based, and strengths-based perspectives while continuing to develop my clinical skills. I believe meaningful progress begins with trust, understanding, and recognizing the strengths people already carry within them.

Above all, I see therapy as a collaborative journey. My hope is to help people feel more confident in navigating life's challenges, develop practical coping skills, and move toward the changes that matter most to them, one step at a time.
